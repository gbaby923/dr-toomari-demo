Meet Dr. Toomari

Dr. Tajav Toomari, MD, FAAP

With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Toomari has dedicated her career to providing compassionate, evidence-based care to the children of the San Fernando Valley.

Dr. Toomari completed her medical degree at the David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA, followed by a pediatric residency at Children's Hospital Los Angeles. She is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics and a fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Born and raised in Southern California, Dr. Toomari understands the diverse needs of our community. She is bilingual in English and Spanish, and her practice reflects her commitment to inclusive, accessible care for every family that walks through her doors.

Outside of the office, Dr. Toomari is a mother of two, an avid hiker, and a long-time volunteer with the Sherman Oaks Little League — where she's the unofficial team doctor for half a dozen youth sports teams.
