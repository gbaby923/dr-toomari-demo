Well-Child Visits & Checkups

Regular wellness exams to track your child's growth, development, and overall health. We follow the AAP-recommended schedule: 2 weeks, 2/4/6/9/12/15/18/24 months, then yearly through age 18.

Sick Visits

Same-day appointments for fevers, ear infections, sore throats, stomach bugs, rashes, and everything in between. Call before 10 AM and we'll do our best to see your child the same day.

Vaccinations & Immunizations

Complete childhood immunization program following CDC and AAP guidelines. We take time to answer your questions and prepare your child to make every shot as comfortable as possible.

Newborn Care

Gentle, expert care for your newest family member — starting with hospital visits and continuing through the first year. Lactation support, sleep guidance, and reassurance for new parents.

Sports Physicals

Pre-participation physical exams to clear your young athlete for school and club sports. Thorough, efficient, and we complete all required forms on the spot.

Developmental Screenings

Comprehensive assessments at every well-child visit to monitor speech, motor skills, social-emotional development, and cognitive milestones — with early referrals when needed.

Behavioral & Mental Health

Caring support for ADHD, anxiety, depression, behavioral concerns, and emotional wellbeing. We coordinate with therapists and specialists when additional support is needed.

Telehealth Visits

Convenient video appointments for follow-ups, prescription refills, minor illness consultations, and behavioral health check-ins — no commute, same expert care.
