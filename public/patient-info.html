FAQs

Questions Parents Ask Most

How do I become a new patient?

Welcome! You can walk in to either office or call us at (818) 205-1666 to schedule. Dr. Toomari personally sees every new patient himself at the first visit. Bring any previous medical records you have — we'll handle the rest.

Which doctor will see my child?

Dr. Tajav Toomari, DO. He is the medical director and sole provider at both our Van Nuys and Encino offices — he personally sees every patient, every visit. No rotating providers, no hand-offs.

Do you offer same-day sick visits?

Yes. Walk in or call (818) 205-1666 — we accept both walk-ins and scheduled appointments at both locations during our regular business hours (Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).

Which insurance plans do you accept?

All insurances accepted — no exceptions. Equal access to excellent care is at the heart of our mission, so your plan should never be a barrier to seeing Dr. Toomari. Call us with any billing questions at (818) 205-1666.

What languages do you speak?

Dr. Toomari is fluent in English, Spanish, and Farsi. Both offices are staffed by experienced, friendly, bilingual professionals so families are always understood.

What should I bring to my child's first appointment?

Please bring: a photo ID, your insurance card, your child's immunization records (if available), any previous medical records or recent test results, and a list of current medications or supplements.

What if my child gets sick after hours?

For true emergencies (difficulty breathing, severe injury, fever above 104°F, or any fever in a baby under 3 months), call 911 or go directly to the nearest ER. Otherwise, please contact us during our normal business hours (Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).

Which location should I visit?

Whichever is closer to home. Our Van Nuys office is at 7100 Van Nuys Blvd #110, and our Encino office is at 16661 Ventura Blvd, Suite 504. Dr. Toomari sees patients at both, so the care is the same in either.

How do I request a prescription refill?

The fastest way is through the patient portal. You can also call (818) 205-1666. Please allow up to 48 hours for non-urgent refills.

Do you provide sports physicals?

Yes — thorough pre-participation physicals year-round. We complete all school and league forms on the spot. Book at least 2 weeks before your sport's season starts.
