About Our Practice

Meet Dr. Tajav Toomari

With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Toomari has dedicated her career to providing compassionate, evidence-based care to the children of the San Fernando Valley.

Dr. Toomari completed her medical degree at UCLA and her pediatric residency at Children's Hospital Los Angeles. She's board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics and a fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Born and raised in Southern California, Dr. Toomari understands the diverse needs of our community. She's passionate about preventive care, early childhood development, and empowering families.
